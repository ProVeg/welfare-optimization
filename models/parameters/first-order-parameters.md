# First-order parameters

These are the parameters §7.1 of [`constrained-welfare-optimization-v2.md`](../constrained-welfare-optimization-v2.md) flags as moving the answer. Full sourcing and caveats are in [§7.2](../constrained-welfare-optimization-v2.md#72-parameter-blocks); this table is for scanning, not citing.

| Parameter | Symbol | Units | Point estimate | Range | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Broiler numbers | \(N_{\mathrm{br}}(t_0)\) | animals / year (slaughter); animals (standing) | slaughter 76.2B→87.3B (2023→2033); standing 10.1B→11.6B | ±15% sensitivity band | McKay (Moulange) & Shah, Rethink Priorities (2025), *Forecasting farmed animal numbers in 2033* | medium |
