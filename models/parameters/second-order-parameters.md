# Second-order parameters

These are the parameters §7.1 of [`constrained-welfare-optimization-v2.md`](../constrained-welfare-optimization-v2.md) flags as shaping secondary behavior (timing, magnitude of side effects) rather than the headline conclusion. Full sourcing and caveats are in [§7.2](../constrained-welfare-optimization-v2.md#72-parameter-blocks); this table is for scanning, not citing.

| Parameter | Symbol | Units | Point estimate | Range | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Species welfare-range weights | \(\omega_s\) | dimensionless (human = 1) — a **vector**, one weight per species \(s\) | pigs 0.515, chickens 0.332, octopus 0.213, carp 0.089, salmon 0.056, shrimp 0.031, black soldier flies 0.013 | chickens 0.002–0.869 (5th–95th) | Rethink Priorities, *Welfare Range Estimates* | low (absolute); needed once species are disaggregated |
| Welfare-campaign effectiveness | \(\eta_W\) | 1 / year | **unknown as a rate**; calibrate so \(u_W\equiv1, u_D=0\) reaches ECC-like \(Q\) in 5–10 yr before ceilings bind | cage-free fulfillment 85–90%; ECC fulfillment much worse (ChickenTrack) | Šimčikas 2019, 2025; Oehlsen 2024; *Limits to Welfare* §1 and §4 | low (rate); high (effectiveness collapses near \(P^{\lim}\)) |
| Density-relief threshold | \(N_\rho,\ \eta_\rho\) | animals (\(N_\rho\)); 1 / year (\(\eta_\rho\)) | \(N_\rho = 0.85 N_0\); \(\eta_\rho = 0.08\)/yr (calibration, not measured) | \(N_\rho/N_0 \in [0.7, 1.0]\) | Dawkins, Donnelly & Jones, *Nature* 427:342–344 (2004); Estevez, *Poultry Science* 86:1265 (2007); Buijs et al., *Poultry Science* 88:1536–1543 (2009); Waterhouse et al. (1996); Yang et al., arXiv:2602.00111 | high (very high density harms welfare); low (\(\eta_\rho\)) |
| Delicacy / residual-luxury exponent | \(N_\delta,\ \nu\) | animals (\(N_\delta\)); dimensionless (\(\nu\)) | \(N_\delta = 0.70 N_0\); \(\nu = 0.25\) (a 50% headcount cut marks up price ~9%) | \(\nu \in [0, 0.5]\) | Lusk and Tonsor, *Applied Economic Perspectives and Policy* (2016), doi:10.1093/aepp/ppv050; Dieci, Mignot, Westerhoff, arXiv:1803.05404; Gallet, *AJAE* (2010b); *Limits to Welfare* §4 | high (supply shocks raise price); low (\(\nu\)) |
| Operational labor ceiling | \(\xi^{\lim} = (N/L)^{\lim}\) | animals / FTE | **unknown numeric value**; qualitative EU evidence of inspection failure (footpad dermatitis 38%, hock burns 20%, breast blisters 6.5%) | treat as a sensitivity parameter, identified so BAU already sits near failure | EU Directive 2007/43/EC and related Dutch decrees, via Dier & Recht (2014); De Jong et al. 2011/2012 | high (constraint is real); low (numeric value) |

| Discount rate | \(\rho\) | 1 / year | 0 (default) | 0–0.04 | None empirical; short AI timelines (*Limits to Welfare* §7) | n/a (value judgment) |
